About

David Samuel · Founder & Director, Phantoma

I'm an independent developer based in San Diego. I'm finishing an Associate's in Computer Science with a game development focus and working through Unity Jr Programmers's Unreal Engine Authorized Training program. Before going independent, I spent time at PlayStation San Diego in QA and on project coordination for the PlayStation Classics catalog — that's where I learned how games get built, shipped, and sometimes broken.

The Studio

Phantoma is both the project and the studio. It's a small operation — me as Director, with contributors brought in for specific systems like character pipeline, environment art, and audio. We build character-driven multiplayer spaces designed to feel lived-in rather than grinded-through.

My Role

Director covers about everything a small studio director does at this stage: design direction, character and animation pipeline, engine work, build and deploy infrastructure, and keeping all the moving parts talking to each other. Day-to-day I'm writing code, reviewing assets, making scope calls, and shipping.
