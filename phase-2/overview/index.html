Game Overview & Instructions

Phantoma · Unity 6 LTS · URP · Cinemachine 3 · single-player explorable build

What It Is

Phantoma is a single-player, character-driven explorable hub. You play an expressive chibi character wandering a lit low-poly city, streets, parks, trees, a clock tower, even drifting clouds. It's a place to be more than a place to beat: closer in spirit to PlayStation Home or a quiet MMO lobby than a combat game. The Final build opens on a title screen, drops you into the city, and lets you walk, sprint, jump, and inspect landmarks at your own pace.

How to Start

  • Launch the executable (or open the Unity Play link on the Source page).
  • On the title screen, press Enter / Space (or click) to begin.
  • You spawn on a city street. Explore.

Goal

There is no win condition. Progression is exploration and expression, jog the streets, find the landmarks (Notice Board, Bench, Street Sign, Clock Tower), read the little stories tucked into each, and enjoy a world tuned to feel calm and lived-in. The HUD shows HP, a stamina arc that responds to sprinting, online status, and level.
